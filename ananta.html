Introduction to Acids and Bases

Acids and bases are two classes of chemical compounds that are very important in everyday life. You can find them in food, cleaning products, and even inside your own body!

auto_awesome

Properties of Acids:

  • They taste sour (like lime juice).
  • They turn blue litmus paper red.
  • They are corrosive to metals.

Meanwhile, bases have opposite properties. Bases often taste bitter and feel slippery to the touch (like soap).
